A coffee table has a job all through December — drinks, snacks, board games — so Christmas decor has to fit on one tray, stay low, and leave at least half the surface free. Style that tray with three heights: a small tree or lantern, a medium piece, and low fillers. Choose LED candles if the table gets busy.

The coffee table is the most used surface in a living room in December. It has to look festive and still work.

## Start With the Tray

### 1. Size the tray to the table

| Coffee table | Tray size |
| --- | --- |
| 90–100 cm rectangle | 35–45 cm |
| 120 cm rectangle | 40–60 cm |
| 80–90 cm round | 30–40 cm round |
| Ottoman | A sturdy tray with handles, 40–50 cm |

The tray holds everything, so you can lift the whole display off in one go when you need the table.

### 2. Choose a tray material

Wood or a woven tray for a warm, rustic look; brass or mirrored glass for sparkle; black metal for a modern scheme. A tray with a raised edge keeps small things from rolling off.

## Build the Display

### 3. Use three heights — and keep it low

- **Tall (20–30 cm):** a mini tree, a lantern, a candlestick or a small vase of fir branches.
- **Medium (8–15 cm):** a stack of two books, a bowl, a candle in a jar.
- **Low (under 8 cm):** pinecones, ornaments, a sprig of greenery, cinnamon sticks.

Keep the tallest piece under about 30 cm so it doesn't block the view across the room or the TV.

### 4. Group off-center

Put the tall piece to one side of the tray, the medium piece beside it, and spread the low fillers into the gaps. Uneven groups look more natural than symmetrical rows.

### 5. Stick to your room's palette

Use two or three colors from the rest of your Christmas decor. If the tree is red and gold, the coffee table should be too.

## Five Ready-Made Combinations

**1. Woodland:** a wooden tray, a 25 cm faux fir tree, a beeswax pillar candle (or LED), pinecones and a few sprigs of fresh fir.

**2. Classic red:** a black or brass tray, a glass hurricane with an LED candle, a bowl of red and gold ornaments, and holly sprigs (faux, if you have pets).

**3. Scandinavian:** a white or birch tray, three white candles at different heights, a small felt or wooden tree, and eucalyptus.

**4. Scented:** a woven tray, a bowl of clementines, dried orange slices, cinnamon sticks tied with ribbon and a candle in a jar.

**5. Book stack:** two Christmas-themed or red-covered books stacked flat, a small brass bell or reindeer on top, and a sprig of greenery beside them.

## Other Ideas

### 6. Lay a small wreath flat

A 30–40 cm wreath laid flat with a lantern or pillar candle in the center makes an easy centerpiece, especially on a round table.

### 7. Fill a glass bowl or hurricane

A large glass bowl filled with ornaments, pinecones or battery fairy lights is simple and can be lifted off in one piece.

### 8. Add a runner on a long table

On a long rectangular coffee table, a narrow runner of plaid or velvet down the center with the tray on top adds color.

### 9. Use a festive board game

Leave a holiday game or a puzzle box on the tray — it looks festive and invites people to play.

### 10. Add greenery that lasts

Fresh fir and cedar last one to two weeks; eucalyptus lasts longer and dries well. Faux greenery lasts all season and can be reused. Keep holly, mistletoe and poinsettias out of reach of pets.

## Candles and Safety

### 11. Choose LED candles if the table is busy

Real candles on a coffee table are close to elbows, sleeves, children and pets. Good LED candles with a warm flicker look almost identical from across the room.

### 12. If you use real candles

- Use heavy, stable holders or a glass hurricane.
- Keep at least 30 cm between a flame and greenery, paper or fabric.
- Never leave them burning when you leave the room or go to bed.

## Keep It Practical

### 13. Leave half the table clear

Drinks, snacks, remotes and games need space. If the display covers more than half the table, take something off.

### 14. Use coasters that match

Festive coasters — cork, felt or wood — protect the table and add a small seasonal touch.

### 15. Store the remotes nearby

A small box or a basket under the table keeps remotes and chargers off the festive tray.

## What Gets in the Way
- **A centerpiece that blocks the TV or faces.** Keep it under about 30 cm.
- **Decorations across the whole table.** Contain them on one tray.
- **Real candles beside dried greenery.**
- **Fragile glass ornaments** where children or pets can reach them.
- **Too many colors.** Match the rest of the room.
