The bunny costume is one of the most consistently popular Halloween looks because it is buildable from almost anything you already own. The ears do the work — the rest of the costume is determined by the colour, the styling, and the makeup. This guide covers 15 versions from classic white to dark and edgy.

## 1. The Classic White Bunny

White bunny ears, a white outfit (dress, bodysuit, or top and skirt), a white fluffy tail, and simple pink nose and whisker makeup. This is the most recognisable bunny costume and the easiest to build.

White clothing is widely available at thrift stores and fast fashion retailers. The ears and tail are available at Halloween shops and on Amazon.

## 2. The Black Bunny

Black bunny ears, a black outfit, a black or white fluffy tail, and dark eye makeup with a dark lip. The black bunny reads as edgier and more Halloween-appropriate than the classic white version.

Black clothing is the most common item in any wardrobe. The black ears are the only thing you need to buy.

## 3. The Pink Coquette Bunny

Pink bunny ears, a pink or pastel outfit, a pink fluffy tail, and soft pink makeup — pink eyeshadow, pink blush, and a pink lip. This version leans into the coquette aesthetic that is trending in 2026.

Add a pink bow to the ears and a bow detail on the outfit for the full coquette effect.

## 4. The Grey Realistic Bunny

Grey bunny ears, a grey outfit, a white fluffy tail, and natural makeup with a pink nose. This version is the most understated and suits people who want a recognisable costume without a strong colour statement.

Grey clothing is widely available. The grey ears are less common than white or black — check Halloween shops or order online.

## 5. The Bunny Ears Headband

A bunny ears headband is the most practical version of the costume because it stays on all night without adjustment. Choose a headband with ears that are wired so they hold their shape.

Floppy ears read as a cute bunny. Upright ears read as a more alert, slightly edgier bunny. Choose based on the mood of the costume.

## 6. The Fluffy Tail

A fluffy tail attached to the back of the outfit — clipped to a waistband, safety-pinned to the fabric, or attached with a tail clip — completes the bunny costume instantly. Choose a tail in the same colour as the ears.

Fluffy tails are available at Halloween shops and on Amazon. They are inexpensive and store easily.

## 7. The Bunny Nose and Whiskers

A pink nose drawn with face paint or a pink eyeliner pencil, and three whisker lines on each cheek drawn with black eyeliner, takes under five minutes and makes the costume read clearly without a full face paint.

This is the minimum makeup for a bunny costume. Without the nose and whiskers, bunny ears on their own read as a fashion accessory rather than a costume.

## 8. The Playboy Bunny (Classic)

A black strapless bodysuit, black bunny ears, a white fluffy tail, a white collar and cuffs, and black tights. This is the most iconic bunny costume silhouette and one of the most recognisable Halloween looks.

The white collar and cuffs are the specific detail that identifies the Playboy Bunny. Without them, a black bodysuit with ears reads as a generic black bunny.

## 9. The Velvet Bunny

Velvet bunny ears and a velvet outfit — a velvet dress, a velvet bodysuit, or a velvet top and skirt — add a luxurious texture that elevates the costume. Choose deep jewel tones: midnight blue, deep plum, or forest green.

Velvet bunny ears are available on Etsy and at some Halloween shops. They are less common than standard fabric ears but create a more sophisticated look.

## 10. The Glow-in-the-Dark Bunny

White bunny ears and a white outfit treated with glow-in-the-dark spray paint or UV-reactive paint. Under normal light, the costume reads as a classic white bunny. Under UV or black light, it glows.

UV-reactive paint is available at craft stores and online. Apply it to the ears, the tail, and any white elements of the outfit.

## 11. The Zombie Bunny

White bunny ears, a white outfit distressed with grey and brown paint to suggest decay, and zombie makeup — pale foundation, dark eye sockets, and one wound detail. The contrast between the cute bunny silhouette and the horror makeup is the joke.

This is one of the most effective mashup costumes because the two elements are visually opposite.

## 12. The Bunny Onesie

A bunny onesie — a full-body suit with attached ears and tail — is the most comfortable and warmest bunny costume option. It suits cold Halloween nights and works for children and adults.

Bunny onesies are widely available at Halloween shops, online retailers, and some supermarkets in October.

## 13. The Couple Bunny Costume

Two people in matching bunny costumes — one white, one black, or both in the same colour — creates a simple and effective couple costume. The matching ears and tails create an immediate visual connection.

Alternatively, one person wears a white bunny costume and the other wears a magician costume — the classic magician-and-rabbit pairing.

## 14. The Bunny With a Twist

Add an unexpected element to the classic bunny costume — a tool belt (builder bunny), a lab coat (scientist bunny), a chef's hat (chef bunny), or a graduation cap (academic bunny). The bunny ears on top of a professional outfit create a playful contrast.

This version works well for office Halloween parties where a full costume is not appropriate.

## 15. Keep It Simple

The bunny costume works because it is simple. Ears, a matching outfit, a tail, and a nose. The more elements you add, the less clearly it reads as a bunny. Start with the ears and the tail, add the nose and whiskers, and stop there unless you have a specific version in mind.

The simplest bunny costume is often the most effective.

## Final Thoughts

The bunny costume is one of the most versatile Halloween looks because the colour and styling can shift it from cute to edgy to horror without changing the silhouette. Start with the ears — everything else follows from the colour you choose.
