Group Halloween costumes from animated franchises work because the characters are colour-coded, immediately recognisable, and available in every age and size. Toy Story 5 and Inside Out 2 are the two leading group costume themes for families and friend groups in 2026. This guide covers both franchises with character assignments, outfit guides, and budget options.

Inside Out 2: The Colour-Coded Group

Inside Out 2 is the easiest group costume framework available because each emotion is a single solid colour. Assign each person a colour and the group reads instantly from across a room.

1. Joy — Yellow

Joy wears yellow from head to toe with a short, spiky blonde hairstyle. A yellow dress or yellow top and skirt, yellow tights, and a blonde wig or yellow hair spray. Add a star-shaped accessory or a small glowing prop.

Yellow is one of the easiest colours to find in thrift stores and fast fashion retailers.

2. Sadness — Blue

Sadness wears blue with a turtleneck, round glasses, and a blue bob hairstyle. A blue turtleneck, blue trousers or skirt, and a blue bob wig. Add round glasses from a dollar store.

Blue is the most common colour in any wardrobe — most people already own the base pieces.

3. Anger — Red

Anger wears red with a brick-red skin tone (achievable with red face paint) and a short, square hairstyle. A red shirt and red trousers, with red face paint for the full effect. A flame prop on the head (a headband with orange and red fabric flames) completes the look.

4. Fear — Purple

Fear wears purple with a bow tie and a nervous expression. A purple shirt, purple trousers, and a purple bow tie. Add a purple wig or purple hair spray for the full effect.

5. Disgust — Green

Disgust wears green with a sash and a stylish updo. A green dress or green top and skirt, with a small sash or scarf. Add a green wig or green hair spray.

6. Anxiety — Orange

Anxiety is the new character in Inside Out 2, wearing orange with a frantic energy. An orange outfit with wild, curly orange hair (a wig or hair spray). Add an anxious expression and nervous hand gestures for the full effect.

7. Envy — Teal

Envy wears teal with a competitive expression. A teal outfit with teal hair. Teal is less common than the other colours, so a teal wig is the most practical option.

8. Ennui — Dark Blue

Ennui wears dark blue with a bored, deadpan expression. A dark blue outfit with dark blue hair. The expression is as important as the costume — Ennui’s defining characteristic is complete disinterest.

Toy Story 5: The Classic Group

Toy Story 5 costumes work best when the group includes both toys and humans. The three core characters are Woody, Buzz, and Jessie — add Andy for the most memorable group detail.

9. Woody

Woody wears a plaid shirt (yellow and red check), jeans, a cowboy hat, and a sheriff badge. The badge is the most important detail — print one on card stock or buy a plastic sheriff badge from a dollar store.

Add a pull-string on the back of the shirt (a piece of string with a ring attached) for the most recognisable Woody detail.

10. Buzz Lightyear

Buzz wears a white and green space ranger outfit. A full Buzz costume is available at Halloween shops and on Amazon. For a budget version, wear white and green clothing and add a Buzz Lightyear chest piece (available as a costume accessory) over the top.

Add the Buzz catchphrase — “To infinity and beyond!” — for the full effect.

11. Jessie

Jessie wears a red and white cowgirl outfit with a red hat and red hair in two braids. A red shirt with white fringe, jeans, a red cowgirl hat, and red hair (a wig or hair spray). The fringe detail is the most recognisable element.

12. Rex

Rex wears green from head to toe with a dinosaur tail and small arms. A green outfit with a dinosaur tail attached to the back and small foam arms held close to the chest. Add a dinosaur headband for the full effect.

Rex is the easiest Toy Story costume for a child — a green outfit and a tail is all that is needed.

13. Hamm

Hamm is a pink piggy bank. A pink outfit with a coin slot detail on the back (a piece of black tape or fabric). Add a pink snout (a small pink circle on the nose) and pink ears.

14. Slinky Dog

Slinky Dog wears brown and tan with a spring midsection. Two people can wear matching brown outfits connected by a large spring or coiled fabric between them. This is the most creative and photogenic Toy Story group costume.

15. Andy

A child in a plain white t-shirt with “Andy” written on the sole of their shoe (in permanent marker, as in the films) is the most memorable detail in a Toy Story group costume. The shoe detail is the reference that every Toy Story fan will recognise immediately.

16. Mixing Both Franchises

For a very large group, mix Inside Out and Toy Story characters. The two franchises are both Pixar, both immediately recognisable, and both colour-coded — the group will read as a cohesive Pixar theme even with characters from different films.

Assign the most visually distinct characters first: Joy (yellow), Anger (red), Woody (plaid), and Buzz (white and green) are the four that read most clearly from a distance.

17. Budget Tips for Group Costumes

The most expensive part of any group costume is coordination. Assign characters early, share a shopping list, and buy from the same sources so the quality and finish are consistent.

For Inside Out: solid-colour clothing from thrift stores or fast fashion retailers, plus a wig in the character colour. Total per person: $10–$25.

For Toy Story: thrift store clothing in the right colours, plus one or two character-specific accessories (badge, hat, tail). Total per person: $10–$30.

Final Thoughts

Group costumes from animated franchises work because the visual language is already established. The colour-coding of Inside Out and the iconic silhouettes of Toy Story mean the group reads instantly without any explanation. Assign characters early, keep the palette consistent, and the group will be one of the most recognisable at any Halloween event.

Still wondering

Common questions

What are the Inside Out 2 character colours for Halloween costumes?

Joy is yellow, Sadness is blue, Anger is red, Fear is purple, Disgust is green, Anxiety is orange, Envy is teal, Ennui is dark blue, and Embarrassment is pink. Each character wears a solid colour from head to toe with a simple hairstyle. The colour is the costume — everything else is a supporting detail.

What is the easiest Toy Story group costume for a family?

Woody (plaid shirt, jeans, cowboy hat, sheriff badge), Buzz Lightyear (white and green outfit or a Buzz costume), and Jessie (red hair, cowgirl hat, red and white outfit) are the three most recognisable characters. Add a child in a plain white t-shirt with 'Andy' written on the sole of their shoe for the most memorable group detail.
