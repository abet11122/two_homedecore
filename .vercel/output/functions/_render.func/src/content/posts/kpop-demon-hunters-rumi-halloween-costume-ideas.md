Rumi from K-Pop Demon Hunters is the breakout Halloween costume of 2026. The look is specific enough to be recognisable and practical enough to build from pieces you can actually find — a pink argyle sweater, dragon braids, dark jeans, and combat boots. This guide breaks it down piece by piece so you can put it together without a full cosplay budget.

## 1. Start With the Dragon Braids

The braids are the most recognisable part of the costume and the one that takes the most planning. Dragon braids are a chunky, sculptural style that sits close to the scalp in a curved or zigzag pattern, with volume built up through extensions.

Book a braiding stylist at least two weeks before Halloween — appointments fill fast in October. If you are doing them yourself, a braid extension kit gives you the volume you need. Search "dragon braid tutorial" on YouTube for step-by-step technique.

## 2. Find an Oversized Pink Argyle Sweater

Rumi's sweater is oversized, pink-toned, and argyle-patterned. Thrift stores are the best source right now — argyle knitwear is common in the men's section and costs almost nothing. Size up two sizes for the right silhouette.

H&M, ASOS, and Shein all carry pink argyle styles in season. Look for dusty rose or bubblegum pink rather than hot pink — the character's palette is soft, not neon.

## 3. Wear Dark Skinny or Straight Jeans

Rumi pairs the oversized sweater with dark jeans — either skinny or straight cut. Black or dark indigo both work. The contrast between the soft pink top and the dark bottom half is part of what makes the silhouette read correctly.

Avoid distressed or light-wash denim. The look is idol-polished, not casual.

## 4. Add Combat Boots

Black combat boots complete the lower half. Chunky-soled lace-up boots are the closest match. Platform versions add height and lean into the K-pop styling.

If you already own black ankle boots, those work too. The boots are a supporting detail — the braids and sweater do the heavy lifting.

## 5. Layer a Dark Choker or Collar Necklace

Rumi wears a dark choker or structured collar necklace as her demon-hunter accessory. A black velvet choker costs almost nothing and is available at any accessories shop. A chain collar or spiked choker also works.

This is the detail that shifts the look from K-pop idol to K-pop demon hunter.

## 6. Add a Glowing or Prop Weapon

A glowing prop sword, staff, or orb signals the Demon Hunters angle without a full cosplay build. Toy stores and Halloween shops carry LED prop weapons. A simple glowing orb held in one hand photographs well and is easy to carry all night.

If you want to keep it minimal, a dark wrist cuff or arm wrap also reads as demon-hunter gear.

## 7. Use Bold Eye Makeup

Rumi's makeup is idol-level: sharp eyeliner, a strong lower lash line, and a neutral or berry lip. The eye makeup is the most important part — it reads in photos and from a distance.

A graphic liner look with a small detail under the eye (a star, a teardrop, or a small symbol) adds the fantasy element without full face paint.

## 8. Choose the Right Bag or No Bag

If you need to carry things, a small black crossbody or a dark structured bag fits the aesthetic. Avoid anything casual or brightly coloured — it breaks the silhouette.

Alternatively, use a small dark backpack worn high on the back, which is consistent with the character's school-meets-demon-hunter styling.

## 9. Do a Group Version With the Full Demon Hunters Team

K-Pop Demon Hunters has a full ensemble cast, each with a distinct colour and braid style. If you are going as a group, assign each person a character colour — pink for Rumi, blue, green, and black for the other members — and match the argyle sweater colour accordingly.

The braids and combat boots stay consistent across all characters. Only the sweater colour and accessory colour change.

## 10. Keep the Costume Wearable All Night

The Rumi costume is practical by design. The sweater is warm, the jeans are comfortable, and the boots are walkable. The only thing to plan for is the braids — make sure they are secure before you leave the house.

Carry a few extra hair pins and a small mirror. Dragon braids can loosen over a long night.

## 11. Budget Version Under $30

Thrift store argyle sweater: under $10. Black jeans you already own: $0. Black boots you already own: $0. Braid extension kit: $8–$15. Black choker: $3–$5. Total: under $30 for a costume that is the most searched look of the season.

The only cost that varies is the braids — a stylist appointment runs $40–$80, but the extension kit route keeps the whole costume under budget.

## 12. Where to Find Everything Last Minute

If Halloween is a week away: ASOS and Shein both ship fast. Thrift stores restock knitwear weekly in October. Amazon carries braid extension kits with two-day delivery. Spirit Halloween and Party City carry prop weapons and chokers in store.

The sweater and the choker are the two pieces most likely to sell out — order those first.

## Final Thoughts

The Rumi costume works because it is built on real clothes rather than a full cosplay kit. The dragon braids are the commitment — everything else is findable in a weekend. Start with the braids, find the sweater, and the rest falls into place.
