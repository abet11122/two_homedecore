The Wicked movie costumes are the second most searched Halloween look of 2026, and the duo format is what makes them so popular — Elphaba and Glinda are visually opposite in every way, which means the pair reads instantly from across a room. This guide covers both costumes separately and together, with budget options for each.

## Elphaba: The Core Three Pieces

Elphaba's costume is built on three things: green face paint, a black dress, and a wide-brim black hat. Everything else is optional. Without the green makeup, the costume reads as a generic witch. The green is the signal.

Use theatrical green face paint rather than body paint — it has better coverage, stays on longer, and is easier to blend. Apply it with a damp sponge and set it with translucent powder.

## Elphaba: The Dress

A long black dress or black skirt-and-top combination works. The silhouette should be fitted or structured rather than flowing — Elphaba's look is sharp, not soft. A black Victorian-style dress is the closest match, but any black dress with some structure works.

Thrift stores are the best source. Black dresses are the most common item in any thrift store's formal section and cost almost nothing.

## Elphaba: The Hat

A wide-brim black hat is the most recognisable element after the green makeup. Craft stores, dollar stores, and Halloween shops all carry them. Choose the widest brim you can find — a narrow brim reads as a fedora, not a witch hat.

If the hat is too tall and floppy, stuff the crown with tissue paper to hold its shape.

## Elphaba: The Cape

A black cape adds drama and photographs well. It is optional but worth adding if you can find one cheaply. A large black scarf or piece of black fabric draped over the shoulders works as a substitute.

Capes are widely available at Halloween shops and on Amazon in October.

## Elphaba: Makeup Beyond the Green

Sharp black eyeliner, a strong brow, and a dark lip complete the look. The makeup should be dramatic — Elphaba is not a soft character. A dark plum or black lip is more accurate than a neutral one.

Set everything with setting spray so the green does not transfer onto the black dress.

## Glinda: The Dress

Glinda's costume is built on a pink ballgown or full-skirted pink dress. The fuller the skirt, the more recognisable the costume. A tulle skirt over a pink dress adds volume without requiring a full ballgown.

Thrift stores often have formal dresses in pink. Prom dress sections are the best place to look. Online, ASOS and Amazon both carry pink tulle dresses at low prices in October.

## Glinda: The Crown and Wand

A small tiara or crown and a wand are the two accessories that complete the Glinda look. Dollar stores carry both. The wand can be a simple star-tipped stick — craft stores sell plain wooden wands that you can paint pink and add a star to.

The crown should be small and delicate rather than large and dramatic. Glinda's aesthetic is refined, not garish.

## Glinda: Hair and Makeup

A blonde updo is the most accurate hairstyle. If your hair is not naturally blonde, a blonde updo wig is widely available and inexpensive. Style it high on the head with curls or waves.

Makeup should be soft and glittery: pink eyeshadow, pink blush, a pink or nude lip, and pink or silver glitter on the cheekbones. The look is pretty and polished, not dramatic.

## The Duo: Why It Works

The Elphaba and Glinda duo works because the contrast is immediate and total. One costume is all black with green skin. The other is all pink with a crown. There is no ambiguity about who is who, and the pair photographs well from any angle.

The contrast also means the two costumes do not need to match in quality or budget — a simple Elphaba and a simple Glinda still read as a pair.

## Group Version: The Full Wicked Cast

For a larger group, add Fiyero (a prince costume in blue and gold), the Wizard (a top hat and waistcoat), and Nessa (a black dress with a wheelchair prop or dark accessories). Each character has a distinct colour and silhouette, so the group reads clearly even in a crowded room.

## Budget Breakdown

Elphaba under $30: thrift store black dress ($5–$10), wide-brim hat ($3–$8), green face paint ($5–$10), black cape optional ($5–$10).

Glinda under $25: thrift store pink dress ($5–$15), dollar store crown ($2–$3), craft store wand ($3–$5).

Both costumes together under $55 — less if you already own a black dress or pink dress.

## Last-Minute Version

If Halloween is tomorrow: green face paint and a black dress is enough for Elphaba. A pink dress and a tiara is enough for Glinda. The makeup and the colour are the costume — the accessories are supporting details.

Both items are available at drugstores, dollar stores, and Halloween shops with same-day availability.

## Final Thoughts

The Wicked duo costume works because it is built on contrast. Elphaba is all black and green. Glinda is all pink and gold. The pair reads instantly, photographs well, and is buildable on almost any budget. Start with the green face paint for Elphaba and the pink dress for Glinda — everything else is optional.
