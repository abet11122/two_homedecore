The trick-or-treat bag is the most important Halloween accessory for a child — it needs to be lightweight, easy to carry, large enough for a full night's haul, and ideally something the child is excited to carry. This guide covers 15 options from the classic pumpkin pail to DIY personalised bags.

## 1. The Classic Orange Pumpkin Pail

The orange plastic pumpkin pail is the most recognisable trick-or-treat container. It is lightweight, has a handle, and is the right size for most children. It is also inexpensive and widely available at Halloween shops, supermarkets, and dollar stores.

The pumpkin pail is the default choice for a reason — it works well and every child recognises it.

## 2. The Personalised Canvas Bag

A plain canvas tote bag with the child's name written in fabric paint or iron-on letters creates a personalised trick-or-treat bag that is reusable and washable. Choose a bag in a Halloween colour — black, orange, or deep purple.

Canvas bags are available at craft stores and online. They are one of the most practical long-term trick-or-treat options.

## 3. The Character Bucket

A bucket or bag in the shape of a Halloween character — a ghost, a witch, a skull, or a pumpkin — suits a child who is dressed as a matching character. Character buckets are available at Halloween shops and on Amazon.

A character bucket that matches the costume creates a cohesive Halloween look for photos.

## 4. The Cauldron Bucket

A small plastic cauldron — black with a handle — is a classic trick-or-treat container that suits a witch or wizard costume. It is widely available at Halloween shops and dollar stores.

A cauldron bucket is one of the most photogenic trick-or-treat containers because it is immediately recognisable as a Halloween object.

## 5. The Pillowcase

A plain pillowcase is the most practical trick-or-treat container for older children who collect a large amount of candy. It is lightweight, holds a large volume, and costs nothing.

Decorate the pillowcase with fabric paint or iron-on patches for a more intentional look.

## 6. The Reusable Fabric Bag

A reusable fabric bag in a Halloween print — bats, pumpkins, or ghosts on a black or orange background — is the most practical long-term trick-or-treat option. It washes easily and lasts for many seasons.

Reusable Halloween bags are available at home stores and on Etsy. They are more expensive than a plastic pail but last indefinitely.

## 7. The Glow-in-the-Dark Bag

A bag or bucket with glow-in-the-dark elements — a glow-in-the-dark print, glow tape applied to the handles, or a bag made from UV-reactive fabric — is visible after dark and adds a safety element to trick-or-treating.

Glow tape is available at hardware stores and craft stores. Apply it to any bag or bucket for a glow effect.

## 8. The DIY Painted Bucket

A plain plastic bucket painted with Halloween designs — a jack-o-lantern face, a bat, or a ghost — using acrylic paint creates a personalised trick-or-treat container for almost no cost.

Let the paint dry completely before using the bucket. Seal with a clear varnish for durability.

## 9. The Matching Family Set

A set of matching trick-or-treat bags or buckets for the whole family — all in the same colour or all with the same design — creates a cohesive family Halloween look. Personalise each one with the family member's name.

A matching family set is one of the most popular Halloween photo opportunities.

## 10. The Themed Bag for the Costume

A trick-or-treat bag that matches the costume — a mermaid tail bag for a mermaid costume, a broomstick bag for a witch costume, or a shield-shaped bag for a superhero costume — creates a cohesive Halloween look.

Themed bags are available at Halloween shops and on Etsy. They are more expensive than a plain bag but create a more complete costume effect.

## 11. The Backpack Option

A small Halloween-themed backpack is the most practical trick-or-treat option for a child who also needs to carry a water bottle, a snack, or a spare layer. It distributes the weight evenly and leaves both hands free.

Halloween backpacks are available at Halloween shops and on Amazon. They are one of the most practical options for a long trick-or-treating night.

## 12. The Wicker Basket

A small wicker basket with a handle creates a vintage, slightly witchy trick-or-treat container. Add a ribbon in a Halloween colour and a small decoration — a bat, a pumpkin, or a skull — to the handle.

Wicker baskets are available at home stores and craft stores. They are reusable and suit a vintage or elegant Halloween costume.

## 13. The Drawstring Bag

A drawstring bag in a Halloween print is lightweight, packable, and easy to carry. It is one of the most practical options for a child who is also wearing a costume that limits movement.

Drawstring bags are available at Halloween shops and on Amazon. They are inexpensive and store flat after the season.

## 14. The Personalised Pumpkin Pail

A classic orange pumpkin pail with the child's name written on it in permanent marker or painted on with acrylic paint creates a personalised version of the most classic trick-or-treat container.

Personalising the pumpkin pail prevents mix-ups in a group and makes the container feel special to the child.

## 15. Safety First: Reflective Details

Whatever bag or bucket you choose, add a reflective detail — reflective tape on the handle, a glow stick attached to the bag, or a clip-on LED light — so the child is visible to drivers after dark.

Trick-or-treating happens after dark. Visibility is the most important safety consideration for any trick-or-treat bag.

## Final Thoughts

The best trick-or-treat bag is the one the child is excited to carry. Start with the classic orange pumpkin pail for young children — it is lightweight, recognisable, and inexpensive. For older children, a personalised canvas bag or a reusable fabric bag is the most practical long-term option. Add a reflective detail for safety and the bag is ready.
